CesiumJS加载示例（请通过HTTP服务器访问）：

const provider = new Cesium.UrlTemplateImageryProvider({
  url: '/data/SPX_XYZ瓦片_原样未合并_20260805/{z}/{x}/{y}.png',
  minimumLevel: 10,
  maximumLevel: 18,
  tilingScheme: new Cesium.WebMercatorTilingScheme(),
  rectangle: Cesium.Rectangle.fromDegrees(108.73409259, 24.47120628, 108.84577845, 24.76033661),
  hasAlphaChannel: true
});
viewer.imageryLayers.addImageryProvider(provider);
